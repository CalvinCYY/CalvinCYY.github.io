Two equal audiences:

- **Hiring managers and recruiters** for industry AI/ML roles in pharma, chemistry and materials. They scan for current role, scope, the kinds of problems solved, the stack, and evidence of shipping.
- **Scientific peers and collaborators.** They look for publications, IMPRESSION, talks, and open-source code.

Success: a visitor understands who Calvin is and what he builds within seconds, finds proof (papers, code, shipped tools), and reaches him on LinkedIn or GitHub.
