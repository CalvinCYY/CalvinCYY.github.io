# Design System: Calvin Yiu, PhD

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Reagent Label"**

Every section of the CV is a paper label stuck to a bottle of amber glass. The page ground is the glass: a deep amber-brown with one soft vertical highlight down the middle of the viewport, fixed while the labels scroll past. On it sit near-white label panels divided by black rules into the grids a supplier catalogue uses: specification rows, slotted entries, a certificate-of-analysis table, a reference list.

Cobalt is printed boldly, as a supplier prints its house colour. Each label opens with a solid cobalt slab, the 18px band fused with the head, carrying the section headline in white. The hero lot strip, the molecule plate, catalogue numbers, the prize-winning talk and the whole contact label are cobalt; everything else on the label stays ink on stock. The type is industrial and compressed: names and section titles in a heavy condensed grotesk in capitals, running text in the same family at normal width, mono only where a catalogue would print a number, value or date. Corners are square everywhere. Depth is a single soft drop under each label, as if lifted a millimetre off the glass.

The live element is the molecule on the hero's cobalt plate: a white skeletal aspirin whose carbons, on hover or focus, pin a leader-line annotation with an illustrative shift, while bonds one and two hops away brighten and pulse outward like message passing. On first load the hero label prints itself once; nothing else animates on arrival or on scroll.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Amber-glass ground, white label stock, black rules, one bold cobalt printed as solid slabs and chips.
- Condensed heavy capitals for names; mono only for numbers, values and dates.
- Fixed-slot entries with right-aligned, deep-linkable cobalt catalogue chips.
- Square corners and a 2px / 1px / hairline rule hierarchy instead of cards.
- One signature interaction (atom annotation with message-passing pulse) and one once-per-load print sequence on the hero.

## Colors

Two materials and one printed colour: warm amber glass behind, cool white paper in front, cobalt as the only ink with a hue.

### Primary
- **Label Cobalt** (band-cobalt): the printed house colour. Fills every label head slab (fused with the 18px top band), the hero lot strip, the molecule plate, catalogue number chips, the prize talk row, the prize tag, the whole contact label, the solid-button hover, the 6px catalogue hover rule, text selection and the global focus outline.
- **Deep Cobalt Ink** (band-cobalt-ink): cobalt at text weight on white. The hover colour of inline links on label stock, and the text of the inverted prize tag (about 9.8:1 on white).

### Secondary
- **On-Band White** (on-band): everything printed on cobalt: headlines in label heads, the lot strip, molecule bonds, dots and annotations, chip text, contact text, route outlines and icon plates. Also the white ground of image frames and the current-section colour in the nav (about 6.8:1 on cobalt).
- **On-Band Mist** (on-band-2): secondary text on cobalt: label-head metadata, talk topic in the prize row, contact route addresses, the molecule formula (about 5.3:1 on cobalt).

### Neutral
- **Label Stock** (label-stock): the ground of every label panel and the solid-button text.
- **Shaded Stock** (label-shade): the highlight behind a deep-linked catalogue entry, bled 12px either side.
- **Ink** (ink): all primary text on stock, every structural rule (including the 2px rule closing each cobalt slab), the solid button fill, the line-button hover fill and the catalogue chip hover.
- **Soft Ink** (ink-soft): secondary text on stock: ledes, slot terms, organisation sublines, talk venues, hint rows (9.4:1 on label stock).
- **Hairline** (hairline): 1px row rules inside entry slots and the composition list. A rule colour only, never text.
- **Glass Deep / Body / Warm / Highlight** (glass-deep, glass-body, glass-warm, glass-highlight): the page ground, a horizontal gradient deep → body → warm → highlight (51%) → warm → body → deep, fixed to the viewport. Glass Deep alone fills the top bar and the mobile menu sheet.
- **On-Glass Amber** (on-glass): text directly on glass: wordmark, nav, menu button, footer.

**The One Printed Colour Rule.** Cobalt is the only chromatic colour on a label. Use it boldly as solid slabs, plates, chips and rows with white on top, never as a second tint, gradient or outline-only accent. Everything else on stock is ink, soft ink or hairline.

**The Three Substrates Rule.** Text sits on label stock (ink family), on cobalt (on-band family) or on glass (on-glass amber). Never set ink on glass, amber on a label, or ink on cobalt at rest.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Archivo variable (with Helvetica Neue, Arial), width axis 62–125%, weight 100–900, self-hosted.
**Body Font:** Archivo at 100% width.
**Label/Mono Font:** JetBrains Mono (with ui-monospace, SF Mono, Menlo), tabular figures on, slashed zero off, self-hosted Latin and Greek subsets (Greek carries the δ in shift annotations).

**Character:** One grotesk doing two jobs by changing width: compressed and heavy for anything that is a name, open and plain for anything that is read. Mono is the catalogue printer.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (850, clamp 3.25–6rem, lh 0.88, 66% width, caps): the hero name; "PhD" drops to weight 500 at the same size. The contact title uses the same voice at clamp 3–6rem.
- **Headline** (800, clamp 2.75–5.25rem, lh 0.92, 68% width, caps, white on cobalt): section titles in the label-head slab.
- **Nav sheet** (800, 2rem, 70% width, caps): links in the mobile menu sheet.
- **Route name** (800, 1.75rem, lh 1, 72% width, caps; 1.5rem under 560px): contact channel names.
- **Subhead** (800, 1.5rem, 70% width, caps, ruled 2px beneath): column heads inside a label (Composition, Papers, Talks).
- **Title** (750, clamp 1.375–1.75rem, lh 1.08, 78% width): entry names. **Role title** (750, 1.25rem, lh 1.15, 80% width): role names in the certificate table.
- **Lead** (400, clamp 1.125–1.4rem, lh 1.4, to 34ch): the hero role line. The contact lede uses clamp 1.125–1.35rem to 48ch.
- **Reference title** (650, clamp 1.125–1.3rem, lh 1.3, 88% width, to 52ch): paper titles.
- **Wordmark** (800, 1.125rem, 75% width, caps, +0.02em): the name in the top bar.
- **Lede** (400, 1.0625rem, to 68ch): the optional lede under a label head.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, lh 1.55): running text, entry prose capped at 62–64ch. Buttons use 1rem at 650 and 90% width.
- **Body small** (400, 0.9375rem): spec values, certificate cells, references, talks, nav links.
- **Meta** (400, 0.875rem): label-head metadata, talk topics, route addresses, footer, composition-adjacent notes.
- **Label** (750, 0.8125rem, 72% width, caps, +0.04em): slot terms, table headers, composition terms, the prize tag. Spec terms use the same voice at 0.875rem.
- **Mono** (400–700, 0.8125rem): catalogue numbers (700, +0.04em), dates, DOIs, stack lists, metadata. The hero lot strip is mono 700 caps at 0.8125rem, +0.05em, dropping to 0.75rem under 600px.
- **Molecule annotations** (SVG user units): heteroatoms in Archivo 650 at 24px, shift values in mono 700 at 18px, atom ids in mono 700 at 16px, formula in mono at 13px.

**The Width-Is-Rank Rule.** Hierarchy is carried by width and weight of one family, not by adding families. Narrower and heavier means more of a name; 100% width means prose.

**The Printer Rule.** Mono is for what a catalogue would print from a database: numbers, codes, dates, identifiers, stack lists. Never for headings or descriptive sentences.

## Layout

The page is a vertical stack of screens, each holding one label inset from the viewport by the same edge (clamp 16–72px) on both sides. Inside a label, padding is the pad step (clamp 20–48px) and column gutters the gap step (clamp 24–48px). A 56px sticky top bar sits above; anchor scrolling offsets by the bar plus 8px. Label heads bleed to the label's own edges (negative pad margins) so the cobalt slab reads as one piece with the top band.

- **Hero:** the label fills the first viewport (100svh minus the bar). A full-width cobalt lot strip, then two columns at 1.12 : 1 divided by a 2px ink rule: name, role, spec table and actions on the left; the molecule on its cobalt plate on the right.
- **Research software:** two columns at 1.5 : 1, each entry a subgrid of media over body so bodies share a baseline.
- **Industry catalogue:** two equal columns; each entry spans three subgrid rows (head, prose, slots) so slots align across the row.
- **Experience:** certificate table and composition aside at 1.75 : 1, divided by a 2px rule.
- **Literature:** papers and talks at 1.25 : 1.
- **Contact:** a fully cobalt label with two equal route blocks.

Responsive steps: at 1100px the hero and certificate stack to one column (the vertical divider becomes horizontal; the molecule caps at 640px; the headshot sits beside the composition list). At 860px the nav collapses to a menu button and every two-column grid becomes one column; subgrid alignment is dropped. At 600px the lot strip drops to 0.75rem and loses its last field. At 560px spec rows stack term over value, action buttons go full width, and the certificate and talks tables re-flow as stacked grid rows.

**The Equal Edge Rule.** Every label sits inside the same edge on left and right at every width; nothing bleeds to the viewport edge except the glass and the bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical and singular: each label carries one soft shadow, a 1px dark lip plus a long diffuse drop, as if paper were lifted off glass. Interactive blocks (buttons, contact routes, catalogue chips) lift on hover; buttons and routes gain a short soft shadow; on press they drop back with no shadow. Cobalt slabs, plates and rows are printed flat on the label, never raised.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Label lift** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 0 rgba(0,0,0,0.25), 0 28px 56px -24px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`): every label panel, always.
- **Button hover** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 18px -10px rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`): buttons on hover, with translateY(-2px).
- **Route hover** (`box-shadow: 0 16px 30px -16px rgba(0,0,0,0.55)`): contact routes on hover, with translateY(-2px).

**The Paper On Glass Rule.** The label is the only thing that floats at rest. Everything inside it, cobalt included, is printed flat; lift appears only as a response to pointer hover.

## Shapes

Square corners everywhere (0px). Form is built from rules and solid print, not containers: a 2px ink rule closes every cobalt slab (label heads, the lot strip), divides hero and certificate columns, opens each catalogue entry and underlines column heads and table headers; a 1px ink rule separates spec rows, certificate rows, references and talks; a 1px hairline separates slot rows and composition rows. Bordered blocks use a 2px outline: ink on stock (buttons), white on cobalt (contact routes). Image frames and the headshot use 1px ink. On hover or focus a catalogue entry draws a 6px cobalt rule across its top edge from the left.

**The Rule Hierarchy Rule.** 2px ink closes a group, 1px ink separates peers, 1px hairline separates attributes inside one entry. Never use a radius or a tinted card to do a rule's job.

**The Slab Rule.** Cobalt arrives as a full-bleed slab, plate, chip or row with square edges and white on top, closed by a 2px ink rule where it meets stock.

## Components

### Label
The unit of the page. Label stock ground, 18px cobalt top border, pad-step padding, label-lift shadow. Opens with a cobalt head slab fused to the band: the white headline on the left and on-band-mist mono metadata (scope and dates) on the right on its baseline, closed by a 2px ink rule. An optional lede follows in soft ink. Two variants: the hero label (zero padding, a cobalt lot strip of catalogue number, grade and lot, then the two-column body) and the contact label (the whole panel cobalt, title ruled in white).

### Spec Table
A definition list of term/value rows: 9.5rem term column, fluid value column, 16px gap, 10px vertical padding, 1px ink rule above each row and below the table. Terms in the spec label voice; values in body small. A hint row sets its value in soft ink. At 560px, term stacks over value.

### Entry (fixed slots)
Every piece of work is an entry with the same slots in the same order: head, prose, slots, optional links.
- **Head:** entry title on the left, catalogue number right-aligned on the shared baseline, never wrapping.
- **Catalogue number chip:** mono 700 white on a square cobalt chip (4px / 8px / 3px padding), unique per entry (CY-01, AZ-01 ...), linking to the entry's own anchor. Hover fills the chip ink and lifts it 1px over 160–180ms; press drops it 1px.
- **Slots:** 5.5rem term (label voice, soft ink) and value, 12px gap, 7px padding, hairline rules. Role and Stack always; Methods when there is one. Stack values are mono, joined by middle dots.
- **Catalogue variant:** opened by a 2px ink rule, 14px / 18px vertical padding, no media. Hover, focus-within or :target draws a 6px cobalt rule across the top (scaleX from the left, 420ms). A targeted entry also gains a shaded-stock ground bled 12px either side.
- **Feature variant (research):** a 1px-ink framed image on white over the body; linked frames zoom the image to 1.03 over 500ms on hover; logo frames are contained with 10% padding.

### Certificate Table
Experience as a certificate of analysis. Columns Period (mono, 7.5rem), Role (role title, description in soft ink to 60ch) and Organisation (600 weight, 30%, department subline in soft ink). Header in the label voice over a 2px ink rule; rows separated by 1px ink. Beside it, the composition aside: a 2px left rule, 128px square headshot with 1px ink border, a subhead, then hairline-ruled term/value rows of skills.

### Reference List and Talks
Papers: reference title, authors in soft ink with Calvin bolded in ink, source line with the DOI as a mono link. Talks: a three-column table (mono date, event in 650, venue right-aligned in soft ink), 1px ink rules. The prize-winning talk is a cobalt row with white text, mist topic line and 14px side insets; its prize tag inverts to white with deep cobalt ink text. Focus outlines inside the row turn white.

### Buttons
Solid label blocks, not pills.
- **Shape:** square (0px), 2px ink border, minimum 48px tall, 12px / 20px padding, 18px icon with 10px gap.
- **Solid:** ink fill, label-stock text. Hover fills cobalt (border cobalt too).
- **Line:** transparent on stock, ink text. Hover fills ink with label-stock text; the icon inverts.
- **Hover / Active:** lift 2px with button-hover shadow over 180ms; press drops 1px with no shadow over 60ms. Focus is the global 3px cobalt outline at 3px offset.

### Contact Routes
Large blocks on the cobalt contact label, one per channel: a 2px white outline, a 30px icon on a white 5px plate spanning two rows beside the route name and the mono address in mist; a call to action across the bottom whose 2px underline draws in on hover. Hover fills the block white with ink text and soft-ink address, lifts 2px and applies the route-hover shadow.

### Navigation
A 56px sticky strip of glass deep with a faint amber 1px bottom rule. Wordmark at left; section links at right in 500 weight, on-glass amber, no underline at rest; hover draws a 1px underline and brightens to white. The section being read is marked (aria-current) in white with a 2px underline. At 860px links collapse behind a bordered Menu button (label switches to Close) into a full-screen glass-deep sheet of nav-sheet links separated by faint amber rules, fading in over 200ms. Escape closes it and returns focus; body scroll locks while open.

### Inline Links
On label stock, a 1px currentColor underline drawn as a background, thickening to 2px and turning deep cobalt ink on hover over 260ms. On cobalt surfaces the hover keeps white.

### Molecule Annotation (signature)
A skeletal aspirin in SVG on the cobalt plate. Bonds are white with round caps: 3.2px at 50% opacity at rest; bonds touching the active atom 5px at full opacity; bonds one hop further 3.8px at 85%, delayed 110ms. Heteroatoms are white Archivo; the formula sits bottom right in mist mono. Each carbon is a focusable button with a 22px hit radius: hover grows a 6px white dot, the active atom holds 9px, keyboard focus adds a 2px white ring at 15px. Activating an atom (hover, focus, click, Enter, Space) pins one annotation, a 1.5px white leader to a mono id and δ value, and plays a pulse: a ping ring scales 1 → 3.2 while fading (620ms), touching bonds flash 8 → 5px and next-hop bonds 6 → 3.8px 120ms later (460ms). C2 is active on load.

### Motion
One ease for everything: `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`. State durations: 160ms colour fills, 180–200ms lifts, 220ms molecule state, 260ms underlines, 420ms catalogue rule, 500ms image zoom.

**The label prints itself (hero, once per load).** A class set in the head before paint (skipped under reduced motion, removed after 2.6s) plays: the hero label clip-reveals from the top (700ms); the lot strip band scales in from the left (620ms at 120ms) and its fields set in at 420 / 500 / 580ms; the name presses up through a clip (640ms at 300ms); the role sets at 520ms; spec rows draw left to right with 60ms stagger from 620ms; actions set at 880ms; the molecule plate reveals from the top at 200ms. Bonds then draw by stroke-dashoffset outward from C2 in breadth-first order (700ms start, 120ms per hop, 380ms each), heteroatoms fade in behind them, the annotation pins at about 1.3s and C2 pulses.

**The Once Rule.** Arrival motion happens once, on the hero, on load. Nothing animates on scroll; every other motion is a response to the visitor.

Reduced motion: no print sequence, no pulse, transitions collapse to near zero, smooth scrolling off.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** open every section with a label whose head is a solid cobalt slab: white headline, mist mono metadata on its baseline, closed by a 2px ink rule.
- **Do** give every new piece of work an entry with the fixed slots and a unique, linkable cobalt catalogue chip right-aligned on the title baseline.
- **Do** separate content with the 2px / 1px / hairline rule hierarchy rather than boxes or tinted fills.
- **Do** print cobalt boldly as slabs, plates, chips and rows with white (on-band) text and mist (on-band-2) secondary text.
- **Do** keep corners square (0px) on every element, including images, chips and buttons.
- **Do** use the single house ease, keep hover lifts at 1–2px with a soft shadow, and keep arrival motion to the hero's one print sequence.

### Don't:
- **Don't** round corners or turn entries into floating cards with their own shadows; only the label floats.
- **Don't** add a second accent colour, tint cobalt into gradients, or set ink on glass.
- **Don't** set headings or descriptive sentences in mono.
- **Don't** add hard offset shadows or glows.
- **Don't** put a small caps line above a section headline; the label head's metadata sits beside the headline, on its baseline.
- **Don't** animate on scroll or replay the print sequence on other sections.
